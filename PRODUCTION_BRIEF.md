## 0. One-line task

**Produce a 60–90 second cinematic, comedic, no-filter hype commercial for the
gaming clan "RED DEVILRY," marketed as a staple within WARDOGS (by Bulkhead),
in the style of the *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3* commercial
"The Vet & The nOOb" (Sam Worthington / Jonah Hill, 2011).**

Reference video: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gb2SNOeHuso`
(Title: *Call of Duty Modern Warfare 3 | The Vet & The nOOb (2011)* — 1:30)

---

## 1. The concept

A grizzled veteran soldier (THE VET) leads a mercenary clan called **RED DEVILRY** —
a devil-themed unit. He is saddled with a gloriously hapless new recruit (BOBBY)
who is comically bad at everything. The joke is the contrast: elite, badass
war-fantasy imagery vs. one recruit who totally ruins the vibe. It ends as a
recruiting ad: *no skill required, just vibes.*

- **Genres:** Military action comedy / game launch hype trailer.
- **Tone:** Cinematic (epic, gritty, dramatic) **AND** genuinely funny, cheeky, no-filter.
- **Not gory/brutal.** Funny PG-13 comedic violence only (ragdoll, slapstick, oblivious disasters).
- **Must feel like real filmed motion footage**, NOT a slideshow of stills.

---

## 2. Reference-style notes (from "The Vet & The nOOb")

- **Juxtaposition comedy:** Serious, over-produced war-trailer energy interrupted
  by a goofy recruit. The humor comes from dialogue + the recruit failing.
- **Pacing:** Dramatic slow build → comedic beat → emotional beat → hype finish.
- **Look:** Desaturated / grayscale world, heavy blood-red + ember accents,
  anamorphic framing, letterbox bars, film grain, volumetric smoke, dusk/dawn light.

---

## 3. Visual style guide

- **Palette:** Desaturated charcoal/grey base, hot blood-red (#CC1F1F), ember
  orange glow, near-black shadows. Accents only in red.
- **Lighting:** Low-key, volumetric smoke, red rim lights, embers floating.
- **Framing:** 2.39:1 anamorphic feel with top/bottom letterbox; slow, controlled
  camera moves; occasional shake on impacts.
- **Texture:** Film grain, subtle chromatic fringing, soft vignette.
- **Typography:** Condensed poster fonts — **Anton** (display) and **Bebas Neue**
  (subhead). White or blood-red, tight tracking, heavy drop shadow + subtle glow.

---

## 4. Characters

1. **THE VET (Sarge)**
   - Grizzled, scarred, grey stubble, exhausted intense eyes.
   - Black combat helmet with a **red devil-horn insignia**; red tactical neck gaiter.
   - Photo-real, weathered. Deadpan, one-line dry humor.

2. **BOBBY (The nOOb)**
   - Young, goofy. Cartoonishly **oversized combat helmet** that's too big.
   - Mismatched, ill-fitting tactical gear. Holds a huge rifle he can barely lift.
   - Dirt/soot on face. Oblivious, optimistic, grinning. The comedy engine.

3. **THE PACK** — Red Devilry squad. Dark tactical gear + red devil insignia.
   Marching in formation through smoke. Serious, intimidating.

4. **GUNNER** — the clan's **Belgian Malinois war-dog mascot** in red body armor
   with a devil-horn decal + tiny tactical goggles. Tongue out. Heroic but comedic.

## 6. Sound design & music

- **Score:** Dark cinematic trailer score — sub-bass drone, deep war drums,
  risers, impacts, "braams," emotional pads, triumphant hero stabs at the end.
- **Comedy SFX** (match the beats): sad trombone ("wah-wah"), record scratch,
  cartoon boing, comedic bell/twang, wind-up + release.
- **Foley:** Booms, distant explosions, dog bark for Gunner, radio static crackle.
- **Final beat:** Rising triumphant hit + crowd applause under the Golden Apollo card.

## 7. Technical requirements

- **Duration:** ≥ 60 seconds (target 70–80s).
- **Resolution / aspect:** 1920×1080 (16:9) for main; consider a 1080×1920 (9:16)
  vertical cut for TikTok/Reels/Shorts.
- **Frame rate:** 24 fps (cinematic) or 30 fps.
- **Format:** H.264 MP4 (yuv420p) + AAC audio; faststart; target ≤ ~50 MB to keep
  it under GitHub's recommendation (or prepare for Git LFS / external hosting).
- **Audio:** 48 kHz stereo, -14 LUFS-ish master.
